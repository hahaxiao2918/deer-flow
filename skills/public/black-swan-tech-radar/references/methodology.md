# Patent weak-signal methodology

## Candidate screen

A weak-signal candidate is unusual relative to the frozen corpus, not globally unprecedented. Screen along five dimensions: route distance, evidence specificity, temporal persistence, independent-document support, and contradiction with dominant corpus patterns.

Use qualitative levels (`low`, `medium`, `high`) for evidence strength and novelty-within-corpus. Never convert these to a black-swan probability. A single patent may be a candidate but must remain low-confidence unless corroborated.

## Falsifiability

Each signal records:

- supporting evidence_ids and the exact unusual feature;
- contradictory evidence_ids and alternative explanations;
- falsification condition that would make the signal uninteresting;
- monitoring trigger observable in future patent documents;
- review date/horizon and responsible scope.

Examples of triggers: repeated mechanism by an independent applicant, persistence in later claims, convergence of two previously separate routes, or disappearance over two declared slices. These are monitoring rules, not investment advice.

Coverage gaps become search_gap_request for the Retriever. Keep the final title and conclusion at “专利弱信号候选”; never claim deterministic disruption, company success, valuation, or investment action.
